# Channel Brief — The AI Forge

This brief governs voice and strategy for every script and edit. Agents read it
alongside the voice guide. When growth-vs-conversion instincts conflict, **this
brief wins**.

## What the channel is

**The AI Forge** — an information and teaching channel about AI tools. We teach
people how to actually use AI for real creative and workflow problems. The value
to the viewer is the lesson, not the product.

## Core principle: promote within the lesson

Products are introduced **within** genuinely useful teaching — woven in where
they naturally solve the problem being taught — **never as a sales pitch**. A
viewer who ignores every link should still leave having learned something worth
their time. If a mention doesn't make the lesson better, cut it.

## Phase: audience growth first (until 1,000 subscribers)

We are pre-1,000 subscribers. **Growth and retention beat conversion.** Until we
cross 1,000 subs:

- Optimize scripts for genuinely useful, highly watchable, retainable teaching:
  strong hook, tight pacing, a clear takeaway, a satisfying payoff.
- Keep affiliate mentions **light, natural, and secondary** to the educational
  value. Mention tools where they genuinely fit the lesson; don't stack CTAs or
  dwell on pricing/sign-up beyond what actually teaches.
- Still honor the 2–4 tool range and the one-honest-tradeoff rule — but err
  toward the low end, and toward usefulness over salesmanship.

**After 1,000 subscribers**, the balance may shift toward conversion (stronger
CTAs, more pricing detail, comparison-to-buy framing). Until then: restraint.

## Voice + standards

Everything in `style/voice-guide.md` and `style/banned-words.md` still applies:
direct, conversational, no hype, one idea per sentence, every fact traces to
`research.md`, one honest tradeoff per tool.

## Future intent (not yet active)

The channel plans a **permanent recurring AI character / presenter** for visual
and tonal consistency and audience familiarity over time. Keep scripts
compatible with a single consistent narrator voice and persona — avoid one-off
framing a recurring host couldn't carry across many videos.
