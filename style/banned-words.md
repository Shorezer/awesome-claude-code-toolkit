# Banned Words & Phrases

Editor scans for these. Zero tolerance.

## Hype words

- game-changer / game changer
- revolutionary
- unlock
- supercharge
- leverage (as a verb)
- ultimate
- must-have
- secret weapon
- best-kept secret
- next-level
- mind-blowing
- powerhouse

## Filler

- at the end of the day
- the truth is
- here's the thing
- needless to say
- it goes without saying
- believe it or not
- to be honest
- literally (when used non-literally)

## YouTuber clichés

- in this video
- guys
- smash that like
- let me know in the comments
- don't forget to subscribe
- hit that bell
- as you can see
- without further ado

## Marketing tells

- elevate your
- take your X to the next level
- world-class
- best-in-class
- cutting-edge (use "current" or just describe what it does)
- seamless (almost always means "fine")

## Why these are banned

Every one of these signals "press release" or "generic YouTuber." Our audience is choosing between us and 100 other AI-tools channels. The voice is the differentiator.

## Allowed alternatives

| Banned | Use instead |
|---|---|
| unlock | lets you / gives you |
| supercharge | speed up |
| leverage (v) | use |
| ultimate | best (with a specific qualifier) |
| in this video | (just start) |
| guys | you / everyone |
| let me know in the comments | what's your pick — drop it below |
| seamless | one-click / no setup / it just works |
| cutting-edge | recent / new (with a date) |
