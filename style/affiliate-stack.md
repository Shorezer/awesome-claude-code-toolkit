# Affiliate Stack

The six programs we promote. Mentioned tools must come from this table.

| Tool | Niche | Commission | Cookie window | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Jasper | AI writing (long-form, marketing) | 30% recurring | 30 days | Best for blog/content workflows |
| Pictory | AI video creation (text → video) | 20% recurring | 60 days | Pairs naturally with ElevenLabs for short-form |
| TubeBuddy | YouTube tooling (SEO, A/B, analytics) | 30–50% recurring | 30 days | Tier-based; push Pro or Star Creator |
| ElevenLabs | AI voice (TTS, v3) | 20% of first payment | 30 days | Primary for voiceover content |
| Semrush | SEO + competitive research | $200 flat + $10 per free trial | 120 days | Highest single payout in the stack |
| ClickBank | Marketplace (many verticals) | Varies, typically 10–75% | 60 days | Use only for vetted, on-niche offers |

## Mentions strategy

- YouTube video: 2–4 tools per video. Primary tool of the topic + 1–3 supporting.
- Blog post (1200–1800 w): up to 6 tools. Use comparison tables.
- LinkedIn (150–250 w): **zero** tool mentions by full marketing name. Drive curiosity to the video/blog.

## Link conventions

- Blog post Markdown uses placeholders the publisher substitutes: `[Tool Name](AFFILIATE_LINK_TOOLNAME)` — for example `[ElevenLabs](AFFILIATE_LINK_ELEVENLABS)`.
- YouTube description: real affiliate URLs, with disclosure block at the top:
  > Some links below are affiliate links. If you sign up, I get a small commission at no extra cost to you. I only promote tools I've actually used.
- LinkedIn: no link in body. First-comment link only.

## Disclosure

Always disclose. FTC requires it; trust requires it. Every long-form artifact includes the disclosure line above, or its equivalent.
