## Notes

- **Screen-record assets (8 shots):** The ElevenLabs and Murf screen captures need to be recorded against live accounts before the edit session. Prioritise the credit/usage meter shot and the Murf audio clipping shot — both are difficult to reproduce from stock.
- **Motion-graphic density:** Nine of the 23 shots are motion graphics (stat cards, charts, comparison tables). If the editor is building these in After Effects or Canva, batching all nine in one session saves significant time.
- **Timecodes are estimates** based on the brief's section boundaries and a 150 wpm spoken rate. Final timecodes will shift slightly once the voiceover is recorded and locked.
- **Tradeoff section pacing (4:30–6:00):** This section moves through four distinct visuals in 90 seconds. The editor should keep cuts tight here — the argument builds and the rhythm should match. Avoid holding any single card longer than 20 seconds.
- **Pictory and TubeBuddy shots (6:00–7:00):** These are affiliate supporting tools. Screen-record assets showing real dashboards will outperform stock here. If live account access is unavailable, official product demo videos (under fair use for review purposes) are the fallback.
- **cold-open audio sync:** The split-screen waveform (shot 1) and the back-to-back clip playback (shot 2) must be cut to the actual rendered audio samples. Placeholder timings in the table assume roughly equal split; adjust once audio is in hand.
